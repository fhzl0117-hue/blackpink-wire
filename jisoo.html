Eleven months later,
she's back.

Jisoo's solo catalogue runs from 2023's "FLOWER" — her first solo single, now past 600 million YouTube views — through 2025's Amortage, the debut mini-album that set the record for the largest first-day sales debut by a Korean solo artist that year. On September 4 she returned with "CLICK," a single built entirely on her own vocals, while quietly assembling her first full-length album.

SEP 4

"CLICK" is out — Jisoo's first new music since "Eyes Closed"

The pre-release single arrived September 4 ahead of Jisoo's still-unannounced debut full-length album (title and date TBA). It carries no featured artist and is entirely solo-vocal, making it her first new music since "Eyes Closed" (feat. Zayn, October 2025). The music video, directed by Grammy-winning director Joseph Kahn, was filmed in Bangkok.

Review

Amortage, revisited — and what's next.

Text only, always — no reposted photos or video frames on this desk.

4.0 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"Amortage doesn't try to reinvent Jisoo — it just finally gives her four songs built entirely around the parts of her voice the group format never had room for."

Amortage (2025): a four-song debut mini-album, signed to Warner Records, led by "Earthquake." NME called it a "self-assured embrace of pop's timeless pleasures" at four stars; Billboard framed it as a "body of work highlighting the star's strengths while pushing to new creative heights."

"CLICK," arriving September 4, breaks from Amortage's sound entirely — an amusement-park concept and a solo-vocal, no-feature production, directed by Joseph Kahn. It's a stopgap single ahead of Jisoo's still-unannounced debut full-length.
