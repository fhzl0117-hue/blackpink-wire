Desk 02 · Review

Revisiting the catalogue, track by track.

Text only, always — no reposted photos or video frames on this desk.

4.2 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"DEADLINE isn't a group album making room for four solo careers — it's a five-track argument that those careers were the point all along."

DEADLINE (2026): five tracks, no features, no interest in being subtle about it. BLACKPINK's first group release since 2022's BORN PINK arrived as a tight mini-album — "GO," "Me and my," "Champion," "Fxxxboy," and "JUMP" (already out as a single since July 2025) — built, by the group's own account, around a sound none of them had tried together before.

Title track "GO" doesn't ease into the comeback so much as announce it: a sub-three-minute sprint that reportedly went to No. 1 on YouTube's global trending chart within a day, on its way to a first-day sales figure Korean trade press called the highest ever for a K-pop girl group.
