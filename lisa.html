SEOUL — SEPTEMBER 2026

SaWaDiKa,
then Press Play.

Lisa's solo run started with 2025's Alter Ego, her debut album stacked with features from Doja Cat, Rosalía, Future, Megan Thee Stallion, and Tyla, and global hits like "ROCKSTAR." In 2026 she returned to her roots: lead single "SaWaDiKa," out September 4, previews Press Play, an EP her label calls her "most personal" work yet, arriving October 23 alongside a Toronto Film Festival documentary premiere and her first Las Vegas concert residency, "VIVA LA LISA," this November.

SEP 6

"SaWaDiKa" tops 100 million views in under 48 hours

Released September 4 as the lead single from Press Play (due October 23), "SaWaDiKa" was filmed in Bangkok and leans on Thai cultural imagery — Muay Thai, tuk-tuks — throughout the video. It logged 70.8 million views in its first 24 hours and passed 100 million by September 6, making it one of 2026's biggest music-video debuts.

Review

Alter Ego, revisited — and what's next.

Text only, always — no reposted photos or video frames on this desk.

4.2 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"Alter Ego is a debut album with the guest list of a career victory lap — and Lisa spends all nine tracks proving she belongs at the top of the call sheet, not just on it."

Alter Ego (2025): Lisa's debut solo album, nine tracks deep, with almost every one built around a marquee feature. Doja Cat and Raye open it on "Born Again"; Rosalía turns up on "New Woman"; Future, Megan Thee Stallion, and Tyla all get their own tracks. "ROCKSTAR" became the era's signature hit, released through LLOUD, the label Lisa founded herself.

"SaWaDiKa," the September 4 lead single for Press Play, is a deliberate turn away from that maximalist, feature-stacked sound — a homecoming, timed with a documentary premiere (Always Lalisa, at TIFF this September) and the Las Vegas residency below.
