Review

Ruby, revisited — and what's next.

4.3 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"Ruby doesn't sound like a BLACKPINK member's side project — it sounds like an artist who spent years waiting for the guest list to be this good."

Ruby (2025): a debut album built on features that could have swallowed a lesser star, and didn't. Dominic Fike, Doechii, and Dua Lipa all show up across the tracklist, but lead single "Mantra" and fourth single "Like Jennie" — her third Circle Digital Chart No. 1 — keep the album's center of gravity exactly where it belongs.

All five Ruby singles charted on the Billboard Hot 100, a first for a K-pop female soloist's rollout. Fallen Angel, the three-track EP arriving August 28, is Jennie's first Odd Atelier release built without outside features at all — a deliberate, smaller-scale follow-up.
